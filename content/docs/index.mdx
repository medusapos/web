MedusaPOS is an open-source point of sale for [Medusa](https://medusajs.com) v2 stores. It is a browser app, hosted at [app.medusapos.com](https://app.medusapos.com), plus a Medusa plugin that records each POS sale as an order in your store. It is built on [TallyUI](https://github.com/TallyUI/tallyui).

**Try it first:** the [live demo](https://demo.medusapos.com/demo) signs you in to a demo store with one click. The store resets every night.

## What it does today

- Sells offline from the loaded catalogue; queued sales sync when you're back online and land in Medusa exactly once.
- Records each sale as a paid, completed Medusa order with stock deducted.
- Takes cash, and records payments taken on a separate card terminal. It does not charge cards itself.
- Takes a percentage or a fixed amount off a line or the whole order.
- Runs a register: open with a float, cash paid in and out, count and close, with manager approval for a large difference.
- Scans with a keyboard-wedge barcode scanner, or searches by name, SKU or barcode.
- Works on a counter screen and on a phone.
- In the live demo, and in the next release: parks a sale to resume later, and edits a line's unit price at the till.

## What you need

- A Medusa 2.21 store, with the MedusaPOS plugin installed.
- An admin user who signs in with email and password, without multi-factor authentication.
- An HTTPS backend URL (or `localhost` for local testing).
- A current Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.

## Status

MedusaPOS is in **beta**; the current release is 0.1.0. Start with the [quick start](/docs/quick-start).
