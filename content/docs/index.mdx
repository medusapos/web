MedusaPOS is an open-source point of sale for [Medusa](https://medusajs.com) v2 stores. It is a browser app, hosted at [app.medusapos.com](https://app.medusapos.com), plus a Medusa plugin that records each POS sale as an order in your store. It is built on [TallyUI](https://github.com/TallyUI/tallyui).

**Try it first:** the [live demo](https://demo.medusapos.com/demo) signs you in to a demo store with one click. The store resets every night.

## What it does today

- Sells offline from the loaded catalogue; queued sales sync when you're back online, and Medusa never records one twice. A sale the store refuses waits under **Orders** → **Needs attention** ([Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting#review-orders-that-need-attention)).
- Records each sale as a paid, fulfilled and completed Medusa order with stock deducted.
- Takes cash, and records payments taken on a separate card terminal. It does not charge cards itself.
- Takes a percentage or a fixed amount off a line or the whole order.
- Adds a bag fee, a delivery charge or a one-off item that isn't in your catalogue ([At the till](/docs/at-the-till#fees-delivery-charges-and-one-off-items)).
- Runs a register: open with a float, cash paid in and out, count and close. The closure is your Z report.
- Scans with a USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner set to type like a keyboard, or searches by name, SKU or barcode.
- Works on a counter screen and on a phone.
- Splits a payment between cash and the card terminal, links a sale to a Medusa customer, parks a sale to resume later, and edits a line's unit price at the till.

## What you need

- A store on Medusa 2.21.0 exactly ([why](/docs/limitations#store-setup-limits)), with the MedusaPOS plugin installed.
- An admin user who signs in with email and password, without multi-factor authentication.
- An HTTPS backend URL (or `localhost` for local testing).
- A current Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.

## Status

MedusaPOS is in **beta**; the current release is 0.2.0, and app.medusapos.com runs the app's latest build. Whoever runs your Medusa backend starts with the [quick start](/docs/quick-start).

Plugin already installed? Staff start at [Sign in](/docs/quick-start#sign-in), then [At the till](/docs/at-the-till) and [Registers](/docs/registers).
