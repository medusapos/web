**Install the plugin** and **Configure your store** are a one-time job for whoever runs your Medusa backend. After that, you and your staff start at [Sign in](#sign-in), then [At the till](/docs/at-the-till) and [Registers](/docs/registers).

To look around first, open the [live demo](https://demo.medusapos.com/demo): it resets every night.

## What you need

- A store on Medusa 2.21.0 exactly ([why](/docs/limitations#store-setup-limits)) and access to its configuration.
  To check, run `npm ls @medusajs/medusa` in your backend's directory. To move
  up from an older version, follow Medusa's [Updating Medusa](https://docs.medusajs.com/learn/update) guide.
  A store already on a newer version can't use plugin 0.2.3. To ask for
  support for your version, [send feedback](/docs/troubleshooting#report-a-problem).
- An admin user's email and password, without multi-factor authentication (MFA).
- An HTTPS backend URL. For local testing, `http://localhost:9000` or
  `http://127.0.0.1:9000` also works; use your store's port. Chrome then asks
  to let the POS access devices on your local network: choose **Allow**, or
  sign-in reports that it could not reach the backend. An HTTPS POS cannot
  connect to a plain-HTTP backend on another machine.
- The hosted app, `https://app.medusapos.com`, in a current Chrome, Edge,
  Safari or Firefox, over HTTPS or on `localhost`. MedusaPOS keeps sales and
  the catalogue in the browser's own private storage; exactly one tab per
  store stays live at a time — see [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting#keep-one-live-tab-per-store) for
  what a second tab does.

## Install the plugin

In your Medusa store's directory, run:

```sh
npm install @medusapos/medusa-plugin@0.2.3
```

{/* Hosted build: medusapos/app@781cd94 docs/adr/0005-web-hosting-vercel.md, apps/expo/vercel.json; plugin 0.2.3 fallback: apps/expo/components/register.tsx:146. */}
Release 0.2.0 shipped with version 0.2.3 of the Medusa plugin, so the command names 0.2.3. The hosted app at `https://app.medusapos.com` runs the app's latest build, the same as the live demo, and works with plugin 0.2.3.

Add this entry to the `plugins` list in `medusa-config.ts`, keeping any existing
plugins:

```ts
plugins: [{ resolve: '@medusapos/medusa-plugin', options: {} }]
```

You can set these optional IDs inside `options`:

- `salesChannelId`: the sales channel for POS orders; otherwise the store's
  default sales channel is used.
- `locationId`: the stock location for POS orders; otherwise the channel's
  first linked stock location is used.
- `shippingOptionId`: the shipping option for POS orders; otherwise the
  location's earliest shipping option on the sale's products' shipping
  profile is used.

Then apply the plugin's database migrations and restart your Medusa backend:

```sh
npx medusa db:migrate
```

If you run more than one Medusa instance, set up a shared locking provider
first, as in [Plugin setup](/docs/plugin-setup#if-you-run-more-than-one-medusa-instance).

## Configure your store

{/* Admin paths: Medusa user guide, docs.medusajs.com/user-guide/settings/locations-and-shipping (and /locations: "Change Associated Sales Channels", "Create Shipping Options", "Create Service Zone"), /user-guide/settings/regions ("Create", "Currency", "Countries") and /user-guide/settings/tax-regions, read 2026-11-18. Region prices: medusapos/app@bc4570a docs/testers.md (Set up this till: "Prices shown are Medusa's own calculated prices for your chosen region"). */}

1. Add the POS origin `https://app.medusapos.com` to both `ADMIN_CORS` and
   `AUTH_CORS` in your backend's environment (usually its `.env` file), after
   the origins already there and separated by a comma. Write it exactly like
   that, without a path or trailing slash, then restart the backend:

   ```text
   ADMIN_CORS=<your existing origins>,https://app.medusapos.com
   AUTH_CORS=<your existing origins>,https://app.medusapos.com
   ```

2. Make sure the default sales channel has a stock location with an address.
   Tax follows that address. If you set `salesChannelId` or `locationId`,
   configure the selected channel and location instead.
   Link only that one stock location to the channel
   ([why](/docs/limitations#store-setup-limits)). In the Medusa admin, go to
   **Settings → Locations & Shipping**, open the location, and edit its
   **Sales Channels** section.
3. Create a shipping option at that location, such as in-store pickup, on the
   shipping profile your products use, or set the plugin's `shippingOptionId`
   to the option you want to use. On the location's page:
   1. Enable **Pickup** (or **Shipping**).
   2. Add a service zone, the area the option serves: in that section's
      three-dot menu choose **Create service zone**, enter a **Name**, click
      **Manage areas**, check your shop's country and save.
   3. Click **Create option**
      and choose the profile in the **Shipping Profile** field.
4. Make sure a region covers the location's country, in the currency you
   sell in:
   1. In the Medusa admin, go to **Settings → Regions**; if no region lists
      that country, click **Create**, choose the **Currency** and add the
      country under **Countries**.
   2. Prices at the till come from that region, so give each product you'll
      sell in person a price in its currency.
   3. Tax is set up separately: go to **Settings → Tax Regions** and create a region for that country.
      Prices include or exclude tax according to your currency's price preference in Medusa.
5. Create (or reuse) a publishable API key linked to your sales channel: in
   the Medusa admin, go to **Settings → Publishable API Keys**, then add your
   sales channel under that key's **Sales channels** tab. MedusaPOS needs
   this to price your catalogue and to see what the channel sells — see
   [Set up this till](/docs/at-the-till#set-up-this-till) for the till's region and
   channel choice on first sign-in.
   The till shows only the products in that sales channel, so add the
   products you'll sell in person to it.
6. If you do not have an admin user without MFA, create one from your store's
   backend directory, replacing the email and password below with your own:

   ```sh
   npx medusa user -e you@example.com -p '<a strong password>'
   ```

   The POS signs in with that email and password.

   {/* Staff invites: Medusa user guide, docs.medusajs.com/user-guide/settings/users and docs.medusajs.com/user-guide/settings/users/invites ("Invite", "Send invite", "Pending Invites", "Copy invite link"), read 2026-11-18. */}

   Give each member of staff their own login: in Medusa Admin, go to
   **Settings → Users**, click **Invite**, enter their email and click
   **Send invite**. If your store doesn't send the invite email, find them
   under **Pending Invites**, choose **Copy invite link** from the invite's
   menu and send them the link to sign up.

## Choose who may use the POS

{/* POS access: medusapos/app@v0.2.0 docs/QUICKSTART.md (Configure your store, step 7) */}

This is optional. With Medusa's role-based access control (RBAC) off,
which is Medusa's default, every admin user can sign in to the POS. To
limit it to the people you choose, on Medusa 2.21.0:

1. Turn RBAC on. Both of these are needed: set `MEDUSA_FF_RBAC=true` in
   your backend's environment, and add the RBAC module to `modules` in
   `medusa-config.ts`:

   ```ts
   modules: [{ resolve: '@medusajs/medusa/rbac' }],
   ```

   Then run `npx medusa db:migrate` and restart the backend.
2. Create the POS roles from your backend directory:

   ```sh
   npx medusa exec ./node_modules/@medusapos/medusa-plugin/.medusa/server/src/scripts/tally-pos-roles.js
   ```

   If your project hoists packages to a workspace root, point at the
   plugin under that root's `node_modules` instead. The script creates
   "POS cashier" and "POS manager", and is safe to run again. A POS
   manager can do everything a POS cashier can.
3. In Medusa Admin, give each person who uses the till one of those
   roles. Super admins keep their access, and `npx medusa user` makes a
   super admin while RBAC is on.

Anyone who is neither a super admin nor given a POS role sees "This
account can't use the POS. Ask the store owner for POS access." at sign-in.

## Sign in

{/* Backend URL: medusapos/app@c027779 apps/expo/app/login.tsx line 41 (the "Backend URL" field), apps/expo/lib/login-errors.ts line 9 ("starting with https://"). Medusa Admin's default path /app: Medusa docs, admin.path. */}

Your backend URL is the address of your Medusa server, such as `https://api.example.com`. With Medusa's default settings, Medusa Admin opens at that address followed by `/app`; leave `/app` off.

Open `https://app.medusapos.com`. Enter your backend URL, admin email and
password, then sign in. If your store has more than one region or
publishable key, you're asked to choose them first — see
[Set up this till](/docs/at-the-till#set-up-this-till). Let the catalogue load while
you are online before selling offline.

## Make a sale

1. Tap the register's state at the right of the header, or on a phone at the right end of the row under it (it reads **Register** until you choose one), then choose **Register 1** and [open it](/docs/registers#open-with-your-float) with the cash in the drawer (the float). You can browse and fill the cart before that. In the live demo, choose **Demo register** instead.
2. Scan a barcode or search for a product, add items and check the quantities.
3. Tap **Checkout**. The payment screen opens on **Cash**; tap **Card terminal** for a card sale.
4. For cash, key in the amount handed over or tap **Exact**, then tap **Take**. For a card sale, take the payment on your separate terminal, tap **Take** and enter the terminal's reference; the POS records that external payment.
5. Tap **Complete sale** and view the receipt; print it if needed.

[Take a sale](/docs/at-the-till#take-a-sale) has more on each step.

At the end of the day, [count and close](/docs/registers#count-and-close) the register. The closure is your Z report, with each tender's counted, expected and difference.

You can keep selling from the loaded catalogue if the connection drops.
The sync bar shows sales waiting to sync and confirms when they have synced.
Reconnect to send queued sales to Medusa.

Open **Orders** to review sales and their sync status. **Needs attention**
means an order was rejected or has a warning, such as stock being short.
Read the order's details: a warning can accompany a successfully synced sale,
while a rejected order needs investigation.
