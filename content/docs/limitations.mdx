MedusaPOS is in beta. This page lists what it doesn't do yet, and what it expects of your store and till.

## Not yet

MedusaPOS doesn't yet:

- Charge cards. It records payments taken on your own terminal.
- Handle refunds and returns.
- Accept Medusa promotion codes (coupons).
- Take tips, email receipts or show sales reports. Receipts are on screen, with browser printing.
- Keep an order note with plugin 0.2.3. The hosted app shows an **Order note** box, but this plugin doesn't accept notes, so the note doesn't reach the order.
- Edit a customer at the till.
- Add or rename registers. Every store has one, **Register 1**, and one till at a time holds it open.
- Support multi-factor sign-in.
- Run as a native iOS, Android or desktop app. It runs in the browser.
- Scan barcodes with a camera. Use a USB or Bluetooth scanner set to type like a keyboard.

## Store setup limits

Set up your store like this:

- Use one stock location per sales channel. With several, Medusa may reserve stock at another location than the sale's.
- Use one shipping profile per sale. A sale mixing profiles is refused.
- You need Medusa 2.21.0.
- Sign in with an admin user's email and password. With Medusa's RBAC on, the user also needs a "POS cashier" or "POS manager" role; super admins keep their access.

## Till limits

At the till, expect this:

- Only one tab per store stays live.
- There is no offline page cache. Reloading or reopening while offline fails.
- The first sign-in and catalogue load need a connection.
- Stock refreshes every 5 minutes, prices every 30 minutes and base prices nightly, not to the second.
- Deleted products leave the till at app start or within 24 hours while it stays open.
- Stock may go negative when an offline sale oversells, with a warning on the order.
- Medusa's reports can differ from receipts by fractions of a cent. The payment always equals the POS total.
- The manager-approval limit is fixed at 500 of the currency's smallest unit (€5.00 in a euro store). You can't change it yet.
- A large catalogue makes the first sync slow, and search too. This was reported in 0.1.0, and the 0.2.0 release notes list no fix.
