MedusaPOS is in beta. Today it does not, or not yet:

## Not yet

{/* Card charging, refunds and returns, and promotion codes (coupons) lines: medusapos/app@91fac0a docs/V2-PARITY.md:77 (Take a card on a terminal: Missing), docs/V2-PARITY.md:96 (Refunds: Missing), docs/V2-PARITY.md:97 (Coupons in the cart: Missing; the plugin still needs a promotion-code mapping), docs/release-notes/v0.1.0.md:44 (no card charging, refunds or returns); docs/release-notes/next.md adds none of them. */}

- Charge cards. It records payments taken on your own terminal.
- Handle refunds and returns.
- Accept Medusa promotion codes (coupons).
- Support multi-factor sign-in.
- Run as a native iOS, Android or desktop app. It runs in the browser.
- Scan barcodes with a camera. Use a keyboard-wedge USB or Bluetooth scanner.

## Store setup limits

- Use one stock location per sales channel. With several, Medusa may reserve stock at another location than the sale's.
- Use one shipping profile per sale. A sale mixing profiles is refused.
- You need Medusa 2.21.
- Sign in with an admin user's email and password.

## Till limits

- Only one tab per store stays live.
- There is no offline page cache. Reloading or reopening while offline fails.
- The first sign-in and catalogue load need a connection.
- Stock refreshes every 5 minutes, prices every 30 minutes and base prices nightly, not to the second.
- Deleted products leave the till at app start or within 24 hours while it stays open.
- Stock may go negative when an offline sale oversells, with a warning on the order.
- Medusa's reports can differ from receipts by fractions of a cent.
- The manager-approval limit is fixed at 500 minor units (€5.00 in a euro store). It is not a store setting.
- In 0.1.0, with about 2,000 products, the first sync takes about 45 s and search is slow.

## In the live demo and the next release

Split payment, customers, parked sales and line price edit are in the [live demo](https://demo.medusapos.com/demo) and the next release, not in 0.1.0. The next release is not published yet.

See [At the till](/docs/at-the-till#in-the-live-demo-and-the-next-release) for how to use them.
