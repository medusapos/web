## Where registers come from

You don't create registers. Every store has one, **Register 1**, and each till chooses it the first time it opens. You can't add or rename registers yet.

In the live demo, each visitor's till gets its own **Demo register**.

## Open with your float

Before taking payment, choose **Register 1** and open it with the cash in the drawer: your float. You can browse and fill the cart before opening it.

The register's state shows under the header. On a phone, it is at the right end of a row. Tap it to choose or open the register.

## Record cash in and out

Tap **Register ›** to open its panel. It shows the sales this session and the cash expected in the drawer.

- **Paid in**: cash you put in the drawer outside a sale, such as change for the float. Enter the amount and a reason. It adds to the cash expected in the drawer.
- **Paid out**: cash you take out of the drawer outside a sale. Enter the amount and a reason. It comes off the cash expected.
- **No sale**: records that you opened the drawer without a sale. Enter a reason. The cash expected doesn't change.

Each one has **Undo**.

## Count and close

1. In the panel, tap **Close register** to open the count. On a phone, this is in the cart view.
2. Tap the note and coin tiles, or type the cash in the drawer. The line under the amount shows what's expected and how far off you are.
3. Tap **Close register** again to see the closure: your Z report. It shows the closure number and each tender's counted, expected and difference.
4. Tap **Done**. The open card is filled in with the cash you just counted.

Between sessions, **Register ›** shows the last closure's figures.

## Get manager approval

If the cash is more than €5.00 over or short, closing needs a manager's approval. In other currencies the limit is 500 of the currency's smallest unit, such as 500 cents, and you can't change it yet.

An admin of your Medusa store signs in with their email and password in the **Manager approval** box. This doesn't sign you out. If you are on your own, approve with your own login.

The closure then shows **Approved by** and their name.

Approval needs a connection. Offline, or after 15 seconds with no answer, it says "Connect to approve, or count again." Tap **Cancel**, which is always available, and count again.

Never save a manager's password in the till's browser. If the browser offers to save it after **Approve**, choose **Never** or **Not now**. If it suggests a generated "strong password", dismiss it and type the manager's own password.

## Finish an interrupted close

If the till stopped in the middle of a close, the register shows **Close not finished**. Tap it to see "The last close didn't finish."

Tap **Finish closing** to complete the close with the count you already entered.

## Move the register to another till

To move the register from the counter to a phone, or back, close it on the till that holds it, then open it on the other till with a new float. Each close gives its session a Z report. Use **Take over** only when the till that holds the register is lost, broken or replaced.

## Take over a register

If a register was left open on a till that's lost, broken or replaced, another till can take it over. It shows which till holds the register, and **Take over** moves the register to this till. The old till's register commands are then refused, and it shows the register closed.

{/* Take-over cash: medusapos/app@v0.2.0 docs/adr/0022-register-v2.md line 42 (a superseded session keeps its sales and movements, never closes and has no Z; the till's record and the owner account for its cash). Each sale is a Medusa order: plugin-setup.mdx "What a sale records". */}

One till at a time holds a register open. With one register per store, a second till can't open **Register 1** while another till holds it; it can only take it over. Sales and cash movements the old till already sent stay on its session, but that session never closes, so it has no Z report. Count the drawer by hand to account for that session's cash. Its sales are orders in Medusa Admin, under **Orders**; a sale the old till never sent isn't there.

A till that lost its local data and opens the same register again picks up where it left off.

## What the store records

The store records each register's sessions and closures. It works out what the drawer should hold from the sales it received. A sale an admin rejects no longer counts in the register's expected cash.

The till's own expected, counted and variance figures per payment method are on **Last closure**, under **Register**. The app doesn't show the store's own figure yet; whoever runs your Medusa backend can read it, as [Plugin setup](/docs/plugin-setup) describes.
