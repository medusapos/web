  payment" (designer, correctness 8): true.** Spec A70 names the card "Split
  payment".
- **The quick start's six "Configure your store" steps don't show in "On
  this page" (designer, docs 8): true.** They are list items with bold
  leads. Spec B70 makes each a numbered H3.
- **No docs page has a single image (visitor, docs 8): false.** Quick start,
  At the till and Registers show app screenshots on the live site.
- **The footer links are small tap targets on a phone (visitor, phone 9):
  false.** Below 640 px each footer link has 12 px of padding above and
  below.
- **There is no way to jump to "Not there yet" (sceptic, phone 8): false.**
  See above.
- **"Not there yet" should be its own full-width section (designer, visual
  7): not done.** Round 43 (#212) put it beside the phone screenshot on
  purpose, and a test pins that.
- **On a phone, the docs table of contents bar shows the first heading at the
  top of the page (visitor, correctness 9): true.** It is Fumadocs' own
  behaviour and is left for now.
- **The lede is long, the hero screenshot is small, the versions are
  unlabelled, and the hero says "Medusa v2" (designer, developer, owner,
  sceptic and visitor): true.** These are with the front desk.
