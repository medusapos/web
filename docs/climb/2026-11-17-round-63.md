## Checked against the source

- **"The closure is your Z report" suggests sales and tax totals (sceptic,
  trust 7): true.** Registers lists what the closure holds, and Limitations
  says MedusaPOS can't show sales reports. The app's button is **Print the
  Z report**, so the name stays. Spec B64 says what it holds.
- **On a phone, the "Counter or phone" row loses the phone (designer, phone
  8): true.** The row shows the first sentence of the card, which names only
  the one-register limit. Spec A64 names both devices in that sentence and
  keeps the limit first (#166).
- **On a phone, the hero's demo and Sign in notes sit below the screenshot
  (owner, sceptic and visitor): true, and left as is.** Round 56 (#260) put
  them there because a phone's first screen was all text. Moving them back
  would trade one complaint for the other.
- **The quick start's store setup has no step numbers (visitor, docs 8):
  false.** "Configure your store" is numbered 1 to 6.
- **The hero screenshot is too small to read, the lede is long, and the
  version badge is unlabelled or undated (designer, owner, sceptic and
  visitor): true.** These are with the front desk.
