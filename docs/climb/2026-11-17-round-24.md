## What is reverted

The sceptic's worst trust defect is the change #131 made. With "so one till
at a time takes payment" gone from the Not there yet registers bullet, the
one-register limit sat only inside the Phone and counter feature card, under
a title that suggests several devices. That half of #131 is reverted, and the
bullet now opens the list (#133). #131's other half removed the repeated
offline caveat. No judge raised that half, so it stays.

#129 (the demo's register in the quick start) and #130 (till and register
defined) were not cited against, and both stay.
