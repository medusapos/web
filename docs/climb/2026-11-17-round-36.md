## Checked against the source

- **The hero doesn't say one register per store (sceptic clarity 7): true.**
  The Counter or phone card, Registers and Limitations all state it, and
  Limitations cites `medusapos/app@v0.2.0` `register-context.tsx`
  (DEFAULT_REGISTERS). Spec II adds the words to the hero.
- **The Registers card's approval sentence leaves out its caveat (sceptic
  trust 7): true.** The card said a large difference "asks for a sign-in to
  approve it". Not there yet and Registers say any login can approve with
  plugin 0.2.3, including the cashier's own. Spec GG drops the sentence and
  says the closure is the Z report instead, as the quick start does.
- **No path for a store newer than 2.21.0 (sceptic docs 7): true.** The
  peer dependencies of `@medusapos/medusa-plugin@0.2.3` name
  `@medusajs/medusa` and `@medusajs/framework` at 2.21.0 exactly. Medusa's
  update guide covers moving up only. Spec HH drops "move the store to 2.21.0
  first" and says that a newer store can't use plugin 0.2.3. It promises
  nothing about a later plugin.
