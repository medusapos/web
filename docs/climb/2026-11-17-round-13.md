## Checked against the source

- **The hero can't be read at desktop width (owner, designer, sceptic): true.**
  The 1216 px screenshot shows at about 600 px, and there is no way to open it
  larger. Spec HH adds an "Open full size" link.
- **The hero's corner reads "v 0.2.0", but its caption says it is the latest
  build (sceptic): true, and the caption undersold the gap.** The demo runs
  `medusapos/app` head `c63f36c`, where `apps/expo/package.json` still reads
  0.2.0. The image shows more after-0.2.0 UI than the two controls the caption
  named: the nav rail (#300), the title bar (#348), the order note (#286) and
  the Checkout button (#299). Spec HH says the build is newer than 0.2.0 and
  why the corner reads v 0.2.0. The phone caption problem (visitor) goes with
  it.
- **"#235" and the ADR numbers in the changelog are plain text (designer):
  true.** `medusapos/app` is public. The 36 other numbers are its PRs (#154 is an
  issue), and all four ADRs exist at `v0.2.0`. Spec II links them. #235 is left
  as it is (see Found).
- **No page says whether a second till can sell while Register 1 is open on
  another (sceptic): true.** ADR 0022 at `v0.2.0` answers it: one live session
  per drawer, so another till can only take the register over, and the old
  session keeps its sales but never gets a Z. Spec JJ.
- **Troubleshooting's "step 7" is plain text (owner): false.** It links to the
  quick start's Configure your store.
- **No screenshots in the docs, and no picture of the cash-up screen
  (designer, owner): true, but not now.** The demo's close screen has changed
  since 0.2.0: verified manager approval (#254), the register side sheet
  (#328), Z-report printing (#270) and the app frame (#300). Screenshots from it
  would document unreleased UI in docs that describe 0.2.0. They wait for the
  next release, or for a 0.2.0 build to capture from.
