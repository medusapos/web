## Checked against the source

- **The changelog's 0.2.0 order note line contradicts itself (owner,
  correctness 7; sceptic, correctness 8; visitor, correctness 9): true.**
  That was #292's wording, and #294 reverts it.
- **No page says where an owner finds the day's sales (owner, docs 7):
  true.** "Take over a register" says a till's sales are orders in Medusa
  Admin, under **Orders**, but the Z report step doesn't. Spec A74 says it
  there.
- **The "Also at the till" cards give no link cue at 1440 px (designer,
  correctness 9): true, and they aren't links at all.** On a phone each row
  links to its docs section (#204, #206). Spec B74 makes the cards links from
  `sm` up, with the same chevron.
- **The footer links are about 20 px tall on a phone (owner, phone 8;
  visitor, phone 9): false.** They measure 44 px at 390 px (`py-3` below
  `sm`, since round 11). They're 20 px only from `sm` up, where a pointer is
  used.
- **At 390 px, shell commands wrap mid-path (designer, phone 8): true, and
  on purpose.** The break after `@medusapos/` is round 46's. Since #293 the
  package name stays whole.
- **The docs have no screenshots (visitor, docs 8): false.** Quick start,
  Registers and At the till show the sign-in card, the register control, the
  payment screen and the closure. The designer's note on their uneven scale
  is fair, and waits for a recapture.
- **The hero screenshot is unreadable and shows "Add order note", the lede
  is long, and the app and plugin versions are unlabelled (all five judges):
  true.** These are with the front desk.
