## Speed fell 0.4, on a slow capture

Three judges took a point off speed. The owner and visitor judges named the
home page's desktop load of 4,072 ms.
In rounds 1 to 8 that load ranged from 1,050 to 2,285 ms. Round 9's capture
was slow throughout: the phone home load was 1,934 ms against a range of 889
to 1,272 ms before. The home page loads first, on a cold connection, and its
images already use `next/image` with art direction and fixed sizes. No spec
this round; re-measure in round 10.

## Checked, not a docs error

The visitor judge said the changelog's "step 7" link goes to GitHub. It
doesn't: it already points to `/docs/quick-start#configure-your-store`. The
real defect is Troubleshooting's link text, "Quick start step 7", which no
heading matches. Spec V fixes that text.

The owner judge asked for "Medusa 2.21.x". The plugin's `package.json` at
`v0.2.0` pins `@medusajs/framework` and `@medusajs/medusa` to exactly
`2.21.0`, so the pages say "2.21.0".
