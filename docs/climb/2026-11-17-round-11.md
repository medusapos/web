## Why nothing is reverted

The rule reverts a change that lowers the mean. Each fall traced to its cause:

- **Visual, −0.4.** All five judges marked the home hero, which round 10 did not touch.
- **Phone, −0.2.** The visitor's point is the header chevron, which did not change.
  The owner's point is new: round 10's "Already installed? Staff start at Sign in"
  link (#76) is an inline tap target under 44 px. Reverting #76 would also bring
  back the "paid, completed" error. The tap target is fixed forward in spec DD.
- **Correctness, −0.2.** The sceptic's point is false (below). The designer's is
  about the demo store's own data, not the site.

Clarity (+0.2) and speed (+0.2) rose.

## Checked against the source

- **The "Set up this till" links go to GitHub (sceptic, correctness 8): false.**
  Both links in `content/docs/quick-start.mdx` point to
  `/docs/at-the-till#set-up-this-till`. The judge read a text dump, which has no
  hrefs. No change.
- **Where registers come from (visitor).** At `medusapos/app@v0.2.0`
  `apps/expo/lib/register-context.tsx` lines 17–20, every store offers one
  register, "Register 1", and no list comes from the server. It is the same at
  head `c63f36c`. In the demo each till gets its own "Demo register"
  (`apps/expo/lib/demo.ts` lines 42–45). Spec CC.
- **Paid in, Paid out, No sale (owner).** These were checked by hand on the live
  demo's own per-till register, app v0.2.0. Paid in took an amount and a reason
  and raised the expected cash from €100.00 to €105.00. Paid out lowered it to
  €103.00. No sale took only a reason and left it unchanged. Spec CC.
- **The hero (all five).** The old desktop image was taken at a 608 px viewport,
  where the app shows only the grid. The new one is the live demo at 912×830 with
  a four-item cart, filtered to Coffee & Tea, scaled to the old file's size. Spec BB.
- **Cost (visitor).** The repos say only that the app is under the MIT License
  (`LICENSE`; README "## License: MIT"), which grants it free of charge. The hero
  says that and nothing more (spec DD). No price, tier or hosted plan is in any repo.

## Left out for lack of a fact in the repos

Listed here under the front desk's ruling. None of these goes on the site until
a repo or a linked measurement holds the fact:

- whether the hosted app at `app.medusapos.com` is and stays free;
- a named maintainer or support channel beyond GitHub issues;
- the large-catalogue figure (ruled off until a linkable test or soak run measures it);
- a supported Medusa version range beyond the 2.21.0 pin;
- what a "POS manager" can do that a "POS cashier" can't, and who may approve
  their own cash difference (still held for the desk with before/after);
- whether two tills can share Register 1. ADR 0022's take-over suggests only
  one holds it at a time; to check against `v0.2.0` before any line says so.
