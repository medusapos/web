## Checked against the source

- **"Not there yet" sits in the side column of the browser block (designer,
  clarity 8): true, and on purpose.** Round 43 (#212) moved it into the
  browser band because the band beside the phone shot was more than half
  empty. A separate section would bring that gap back. It stays.
- **The phone header has an unlabelled chevron (visitor, phone 9): partly
  true.** It is Fumadocs' menu toggle, with `aria-label="Toggle Menu"`.
  Changing the icon needs a header slot override. Not this round.
- **At 390 px the Sign-in line is two screens below the buttons (owner,
  phone 8): true, and on purpose.** That fine print follows the screenshot
  below `lg`, so a phone's first screen shows the till. This round's designer
  and sceptic both ask for less text on that screen, not more.
- **The one-register limit appears three times on home, once as the
  "Counter or phone" card (sceptic, clarity 8): true.** The card's first
  sentence was chosen in round 64 (#279) to name both devices and the limit
  together. It stays.
- **The receipt in At the till is a table, not a screenshot (designer, docs
  8): true.** A receipt shot needs a finished sale on the shared demo, which
  a capture can't make without opening its register. It waits.
- **The hero screenshot is unreadable at 1440 px, the lede is long, and the
  app and plugin versions are unlabelled (all five judges): true.** These are
  with the front desk.
