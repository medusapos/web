## Checked against the source

- **Limitations is slow on desktop: a median load of 4661 ms and an LCP
  of 2920 ms (four judges, speed 8): false.** Five fresh loads each, all
  served from the Vercel cache, gave Limitations a median load of about
  1590 ms and an LCP of about 780 ms. That is the same as Registers and
  Troubleshooting. Two TTFB spikes in three samples moved the median. The
  capture now takes five samples.
- **The docs screenshots are too small to read on a phone (designer and
  owner, phone 8): partly.** A tap opens each one full size, but nothing
  said so. Spec A52 adds a muted "Tap to enlarge" line under each
  screenshot below 640 px. A52b moves the line up to sit 8 px under its
  image.
- **On a phone, "Also at the till" lists bare titles (sceptic, phone 8;
  owner and visitor in round 51): true.** Spec B52 shows the first
  sentence of each existing description under its title. The phone page
  grows by 232 px.
- **No screenshot shows the Register control (visitor, docs 8): false.**
  Quick start shows it in "Make a sale".
